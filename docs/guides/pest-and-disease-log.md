# Pest & Disease Log

Spotting a problem early is the difference between a treated patch and a
lost field. The Pest & Disease Log is a place to record what you saw, how
bad it is, and what you did about it — with a photo or voice note if that's
faster than typing.

## Where to find it

**Pest & Disease** in the left-hand menu, under Activities. It lists every
incident you've reported, newest first, filterable by type and status.

## Reporting an incident

1. Click **Report incident**.
2. Choose the crop planting it's on, the type (pest / disease / other), and
   how severe it looks.
3. Describe what you're seeing in your own words.
4. Optionally add a photo of the damage or a voice note — useful when
   you're standing in the field with muddy hands. Accepted formats: JPG,
   PNG, WEBP for photos; MP3, M4A, OGG for voice notes. Up to 8 MB.
5. Save. It's logged as **Open**.

## Following up

Open the incident again any time to:
- Add treatment notes (what you sprayed, what the vet recommended, etc.)
- Note who you referred it to, if you called in a vet or extension officer
- Move it from **Open → Treated → Resolved** as you act on it
- Add more photos or voice notes as the situation develops — attachments
  are additive, nothing gets overwritten

## Outbreak alerts

If you report 3 or more incidents of the *same type* (e.g. three separate
pest reports) within 14 days, Ulimi flags it as a possible outbreak in your
notifications — a nudge to walk the rest of the farm rather than just
treating the spots you've already found.

## Livestock too

The same photo/voice attachment is available on an animal's **Health**
record (Livestock → an animal → Health → + Add) — handy for a wound photo
or a vet's verbal diagnosis you want on file.
