# Sharing Reports & Climate Events

## Sending a report pack without giving out your login

Your **Reports** page has four ready-made packs — Loan-readiness, Buyer
evidence, Audit file, Insurance file. Previously the only way to show one
to someone outside your farm was to let them log in as you. Now, if you're
the farm owner, open any pack and use **Share this pack**:

1. Click **Create share link**. You'll get a link once — copy it right
   away, it can't be shown again (though you can always make a new one).
2. Send the link to your lender, buyer, or NGO contact by whatever means
   is convenient — WhatsApp, email, SMS.
3. They open it in any browser, no account needed. They see exactly that
   pack, nothing else on your farm.
4. The link works for 14 days. You can **Revoke** it at any time from the
   same page — it stops working immediately.

Only the farm owner can create or revoke share links, since this is the
one thing in Ulimi that shows your data to someone without a login.

## Climate events

**Climate Events** in the menu is where you log droughts, floods, storms,
frost, hail, or other weather losses — separate from day-to-day weather
alerts, this is a permanent record for insurance claims or lender
conversations. For each event, record the type, date range, what happened,
an estimated loss amount, which planting it affected (if specific), and
a photo if you have one.

Anything you log here shows up automatically in your **Insurance file**
record pack — nothing extra to do.

## Loan pack now shows a summary, not a raw list

The Loan-readiness pack's finance section now shows income and expenses
totalled by category instead of a long line-by-line list — easier for a
lender to scan. The Audit file still shows the full transaction list,
since that's what an audit actually needs.
