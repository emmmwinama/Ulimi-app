# Reminders & Alerts

Ulimi watches your farm records and lets you know when something needs
attention — a harvest coming up, a field that's gone quiet, stock running
low, an animal due for a check-up, or weather worth planning around. You
don't have to set any of this up — it works from the records you're already
keeping.

## Where to find your alerts

Click the bell icon in the top bar. A red badge shows how many are unread.
The Notifications page lists the newest first; click one to jump straight
to the record it's about, or use **Mark all read** to clear the badge.

New alerts are checked for every time you open the Notifications page —
there's nothing to refresh or wait for.

## What triggers an alert

- **Harvest due soon** — an active crop is within 7 days of its expected
  harvest date and you haven't logged a yield for it yet.
- **No recent activity** — a crop has had no logged activity (weeding,
  spraying, fertilising, etc.) in the last 3 weeks. This one reappears
  weekly for as long as the field stays quiet, so it won't get lost.
- **Low stock** — a stock item has dropped to or below the reorder level
  you set for it. Set this from **Inventory → Add/Edit item → "Low-stock
  alert below"**. Leave it blank on any item you don't want alerts for.
- **Sold below market price** — a sale in the last 14 days was priced more
  than 15% under the current market average for that item, based on the
  reference prices on the Market page.
- **Livestock follow-up due** — an animal's most recent health record
  (vaccination, deworming, treatment, check-up) has a follow-up date that's
  due or overdue within the next 7 days.
- **Weather alert** — today's or this week's forecast crosses a threshold
  worth acting on: heavy rain (hold off spraying), strong wind, high heat,
  frost risk overnight, a dry spell (consider irrigation), or sustained
  heavy rain across several days (flood/drainage risk). Everyday "good
  spraying weather" notes show up on the Weather page itself, not as an
  alert — alerts are reserved for the ones worth acting on.

## Tips

- An alert only appears once per underlying event — you won't get the same
  one twice, and most clear themselves once you act (log the yield, log an
  activity, restock, update the health record).
- "No recent activity" and "Low stock" (while it stays low) resurface on a
  weekly cycle rather than disappearing forever, so an ongoing issue
  doesn't go quiet.
- No alerts yet? Check that your farm's location is set (Settings → Farm)
  — weather alerts need it — and that your stock items have a low-stock
  threshold set if you want those.
