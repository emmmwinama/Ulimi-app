# Inventory & Equipment

## Tracking stock in more detail

When you add or edit a stock item (seed, fertiliser, chemicals, harvested
produce), you can now record:

- **Low-stock alert below** — a quantity threshold. Drop to or below it and
  you'll get a notification. Leave it blank to skip alerts for that item.
- **Batch / lot number** — useful for chemicals and seed where you need to
  trace a specific batch back to a supplier if there's ever a problem.
- **Expiry date** — shows as a badge on the item (amber inside 30 days,
  red once passed) and triggers a notification the same way low stock does.
- **Supplier** and **supplier contact** — so you know who to call to
  reorder without digging through old receipts.

All four are optional — add what's useful for that item and skip the rest.

## Equipment

Equipment (tractors, irrigation kit, tools, vehicles) doesn't behave like
consumable stock — it doesn't get "sold" or run out — so it has its own
section: **Equipment** in the left-hand menu.

For each piece of equipment you can record:
- Category, status (Active / Under repair / Retired), when and for how
  much you acquired it
- A running **maintenance history** — date, what was done, cost, and hours
  used if you track that — with a running total maintenance cost shown on
  the equipment page

If you'd previously logged a tractor or similar as an "Equipment" category
stock item in Inventory, that record still works — it just won't have a
maintenance log. New equipment should go in the dedicated Equipment
section instead.

## Alerts

Both low-stock and expiring-stock alerts show up the same way as your
other notifications — see [Reminders & Alerts](reminders-and-alerts.md).
