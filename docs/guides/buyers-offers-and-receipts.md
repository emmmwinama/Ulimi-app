# Buyers, Offers & Receipts

## Your buyer contact book

**Market prices → Buyers & Offers** keeps a private list of buyers you
deal with — this is not a public marketplace, nobody outside your farm
account can see it. For each buyer you can save a name, type (individual,
company, cooperative), phone, email, and location.

## Tracking offers

Record what a buyer has offered you — crop, quantity wanted, price, and an
expiry date if it's time-limited. Move an offer through **Open → Accepted
/ Declined → Expired** as things develop. Offers aren't tied to a sale
automatically; they're a record of what's been discussed so you don't lose
track of who offered what.

## Selling with a saved buyer

When you record a sale from **Inventory → Sell**, if you've saved a buyer
you can pick them from a dropdown instead of retyping their name — or just
type a one-off name if it's not worth saving them. Either way, the buyer
shows up on that sale's record.

## Payment status

Every Finance transaction now has a payment status — **Paid**, **Partially
paid**, or **Unpaid** — shown as a badge on the transactions list. Existing
transactions default to Paid (money already recorded as moved); use
Unpaid/Partial going forward for income you're still owed or expenses you
haven't settled yet.

## Printable receipts

Every recorded sale has a **Receipt** link (on the item's Sell page, in
its sale history). It opens a clean, printable page — use your browser's
Print → Save as PDF to get a PDF copy, no extra software needed.
