# Cooperatives

Farming cooperatives let several farms pool inventory, production, and
sales to get the scale that unlocks better input prices, transport, and
buyers. **Cooperatives** in the menu is where your farm can create or join
one.

## Creating a cooperative

Fill in a name (and region, optionally) and submit — your farm becomes
its **chair**. You'll get a **join code**: an 8-character code you share
with other farms so they can join. There's no approval step — anyone with
the code joins immediately as a **member**. As chair, you can remove a
member farm later if needed.

## Joining a cooperative

Enter the join code someone shared with you and you're in.

## What you get once you're in a group

- **Group inventory** — combined stock levels by category, across every
  member farm.
- **Group production** — combined harvest volumes by crop over the last
  12 months, across every member farm.
- **Members list** — who's in the group and their role (chair, secretary,
  treasurer, member).
- **Contributions ledger** — chair/secretary/treasurer can record what
  each member farm has paid in (membership dues, an input fund,
  equipment fund, etc.).
- **Collective sales** — record a sale made on behalf of the group (total
  quantity, price, buyer), optionally split by quantity and amount across
  the member farms that contributed to it.

Only the chair, secretary, or treasurer can remove members, record
contributions, or log a collective sale — a plain member can view
everything but not make changes.

## What this isn't (yet)

This is a first version focused on the essentials: membership, a shared
view of the group's numbers, contributions, and collective sales. Shared
equipment booking calendars and automatic reports for lenders/NGOs aren't
built yet — those are reasonable next additions if a group finds it needs
them.
